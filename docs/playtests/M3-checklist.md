# M3 playtest checklist (cover and hull breach)

Open `index.html`. Press 1, 2 or 3 (top row) to jump to room 1 (intro), room 2 (cover deck) or room 3 (breach hall). R restarts the room. F1 shows FPS and enemy states.
Rate each item 1 (bad) to 5 (great).

1. **Half cover.** In room 2, press C next to a half-cover block (or dash into it) so you crouch behind it. Stand where a grunt can see you. Do its shots stop at the block? Now hold right click and left click to pop out: do you clearly become exposed while you shoot?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
2. **Full cover.** Take cover behind the full pillar (F) in room 2. Do bullets never reach you? Hold right click: does your character lean out to the pillar's edge to fire, and can you tell when the gun is ready?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
3. **Shield icon and red glow.** With an enemy alerted, watch the icon above your head. Is it green when protected and red when exposed? Does the red glow on the cover edge facing the enemy help you pick which side to peek from? Is either too subtle or too noisy?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
4. **Destructible crates.** Shoot a crate. It should break in 2 to 3 blasts. Do the cracks show damage? Does it feel fair that your own cover dies when enemies shoot it?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
5. **Breach it.** In room 3, shoot a window while enemies bunch under it. Does the opening feel dramatic (shake, sound, wind streaks)? Do enemies get pulled and spaced, and do you understand why they died?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
6. **Breach risk.** Stand near the window when it blows. Can you escape on foot, and is the pull strong enough to feel dangerous without being unfair? Try again from the far side of the room.
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
7. **Shutter and loose crates.** After a breach, does the shutter seal at about 2.5 s? Do crates in the radius slide toward the hole and vanish? Is the seal readable (sound and shake)?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
8. **Clear room 3 using only the breach** (one shot at the window, no shots at enemies). Is it possible, and how often does it work?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
9. **Die 5 times in a row** in rooms 2 and 3 (walk into bullets or the breach). Does the restart ever feel slow? Are broken crates and the window back exactly as they started?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
10. **Room jump and speed.** Press 1, 2, 3 repeatedly. Do the rooms load cleanly with no leftover blood, bullets or breach effects? Note your fastest clear in each room (target 2 to 5 s of active fighting). Room 2's gunners (n) do not exist yet, so only its grunts appear.
