# M5 playtest checklist (flight run into boarding)

Open `index.html`. The run should start in the flight scene (A/D strafe, W/S change altitude band, hold left click to fire, R restarts from the last checkpoint). Debug keys: N warps to the next checkpoint, H toggles god mode, F2 jumps to flight, F4 to boarding room 1.
If the screen is blank, open the console (F12) and paste any red errors back first.
Rate each item 1 (bad) to 5 (great).

1. **First 10 seconds.** Without reading anything, can you tell which way you are flying, what the three altitude bands are, and where your ship is relative to the floor (drop shadow, band colours on the frame and gauge)?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
2. **Reading the oversized gaps.** In the first four walls, can you see which band has the opening before you reach it? Does the gap feel generous (2 to 3 times the ship) or still fiddly? Did you ever die to a wall you thought you cleared?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
3. **Lateral windows.** Walls at 14 s, 16.5 s and 31 s have a window off-centre. Is it clear you must change band and strafe? Is the shadow enough to judge your lateral position?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
4. **Force fields, turrets, fighters.** Shoot out a field generator; are the pylon and generator easy to spot and reach? Do turret shots (slow, orange) read as dodgeable? Do fighters (weave, dive, cross) feel fair or cheap?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
5. **Die 5 times in a row** (fly into a wall on purpose, or use a turret). Does the checkpoint restart ever feel slow, and does it put you at the last green checkpoint banner (about every 20 s) with the course reset correctly?
