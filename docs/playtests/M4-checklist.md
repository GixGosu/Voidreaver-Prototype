# M4 playtest checklist (compartment chain, doors, scanner, upgrades, gunner, heavy, grades)

Open `index.html`. The run starts in room 1 and continues through rooms 2 to 5 via the exit doors. Top-row keys 1 to 5 jump to a room, R restarts the room, F1 shows FPS and enemy states.
If the screen is blank or nothing moves, open the browser console (F12) and paste any red errors back first.
Rate each item 1 (bad) to 5 (great).

1. **Play the whole chain, rooms 1 to 5, without using the number keys.** Do the doors always take you to the next room? Does the run-complete screen appear after room 5 and does R / click start a fresh run?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
2. **Door sealing and the scanner.** On entering a room, does the entry door visibly seal behind you and the scan (about 0.75 s, world frozen, red rings on enemies) give you enough to plan? Is it too long, too short or too easy to miss?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
3. **Exit reward previews.** In rooms 1 to 4, can you tell what each of the two doors offers (ATK / MOV / DEF) before you choose? Did the upgrade you were offered after the next room match the door you took?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
4. **Upgrade screen.** Pick with keys 1 to 3 once and with the mouse once. Is the pause clear, are the cards readable, and is "pick 1 of 3" a real decision? Which upgrade did you want most, and which felt useless? Room 5 also shows a pick screen (room 4's door reward) before its exit opens.
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
5. **Upgrades last the run.** After picking Extra Shells, Hull Plating or Third Charge, die and restart: is the effect still there, and is the shield restored each attempt?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
6. **Gunners (rooms 2, 4, 5).** Do they move between cover, fire bursts, and pin you while you are behind cover? Can you read their wind-up flash? Is a burst fair to dodge with a dash, and is flanking worth it?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
7. **Heavy (room 5).** Does it clearly take 3 shotgun hits (the counter above its head)? Does a kick shove it without stunning it? Is its shotgun blast telegraphed enough to dodge, and is it fun to fight or just a sponge?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
8. **Grades.** Clear each room fast with chained kills, then slowly. Does S / A / B / C show on clear and feel earned? Which room was hardest to get an S in?
   Rating: _/5   Notes: grades per room =
9. **Die 5 times in a row in room 5** (heavy, gunners and rushers together). Does restart ever feel slow or unfair? Do the heavy's hits, your shells and any broken window or crate come back exactly as at the start?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
10. **Crowd and speed.** Does the game hold 60 fps (F1) in room 5 with everyone shooting? Note your fastest active-fight clear per room (target 2 to 5 s) and any room that took more than 8 s or more than 6 attempts.
