# M2 playtest checklist (lethal combat: shotgun, grunt, rusher, instant restart)

Open `index.html` by double-clicking it. Rate each item 1 (bad) to 5 (great). Press F1 to see FPS and enemy states.
The automated smoke test passes for M2 (combat modules load, 20 enemies hold 60 fps).

1. **Shotgun punch.** Fire at a grunt from point blank, then from the edge of your range. Does one blast always kill? Does the body fly back and slide in a way that feels brutal? Does the screen shake and the tiny freeze on the kill read as a hit, not lag?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
2. **Knockback into walls.** Shoot enemies standing near a wall so they are thrown into it. Is it clear that the wall kill happened, and does it feel like a reward?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
3. **Grunt telegraph.** Walk into a grunt's line of sight and stand still. Can you see the grunt react before the first shot (about 0.35 s)? Is the shot ever unfair, meaning you died with no warning? Then try to dodge the bullet with a dash.
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
4. **Rusher pressure.** Let a rusher spot you from across the room. Does the charge read early enough to shoot or dash away? Is the shotgun fast enough (0.45 s pump) to deal with two rushers in a row?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
5. **Die 5 times in a row.** Walk into bullets on purpose five times. Does the freeze plus restart ever feel slow? Is the room back exactly as it started (enemy positions, shells, no leftover blood)? Does the camera snap cleanly?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
6. **Shell budget.** Fire at nothing until the shotgun is empty. Is it obvious you are out of shells? Try G to throw the empty gun, and pick up a pistol dropped by a grunt. Does the swap feel quick?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
7. **Sounds and splatter.** With sound on, listen for shot, kill, death and room clear. Are they distinct and not harsh? Do the decals stay on the floor and look right after a few kills?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
8. **Combo and grade.** Kill two or more enemies within 1.5 s of each other. Does the combo counter appear? On room clear, is the grade (S/A/B/C) shown and does it seem fair for how you played?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
9. **Speed target.** Clear the 4-enemy room as fast as you can, three attempts. Write down your fastest active fight time. Spec target is 2 to 5 s once you know the layout.
