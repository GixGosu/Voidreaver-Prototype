# M1 playtest checklist (skeleton: walk and aim)

Open `index.html` by double-clicking it. Rate each item 1 (bad) to 5 (great).

1. **Walk in all 8 directions.** W should go up the screen, D right, and so on. Does movement feel screen-relative and snappy, with no sluggish start or stop?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
2. **Aim accuracy.** Press F1 and move the cursor around the whole room, including near walls and the top and bottom corners. Does the yellow tile outline always sit exactly under the cursor, and does the aim line end at the reticle?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
3. **Wall sliding.** Run diagonally into walls, the block of cover in the middle and the corner by the bottom-left. Do you slide along them smoothly, or do you snag or get stuck?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
4. **Depth sorting.** Walk behind and in front of the crates, half cover, full cover and the inner wall block. Does the player ever draw on the wrong side of an object?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
5. **Wall fade.** Walk behind the tall walls and pillars at the top of the room. Do the walls hiding you turn to outlines so you can still see yourself? Is the change readable and not jarring?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
6. **Camera lead.** Move the cursor far to one side while standing still, then walk. Does the camera lean toward the aim without making you feel seasick, and can you still see around you?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
7. **Frame rate.** With F1 on, run around for 30 seconds. Does FPS stay near 60 with no hitches? Write down the lowest FPS you saw and your machine and browser.
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
8. **Restart.** Press R after walking away from the start. Is the reset instant, and does the camera snap without a swoop?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
9. **Resize and focus.** Resize the window, then alt-tab away and back. Does the view rescale, and do keys ever stay stuck?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:
10. **Readability.** Is the room roomy enough that you can tell floor, wall, cover and window tiles apart at a glance? What is hardest to read?
   Rating: _/5   Notes:

When done, paste any notes back. No telemetry export exists yet (it arrives in M6).
